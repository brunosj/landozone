#### 3. Web analytics (Plausible)

We use **Plausible Analytics** in a **self-hosted** instance at `plausible.landozone.net`, running on our own infrastructure in Germany.

Plausible is designed as privacy-friendly analytics: it does **not** use cookies, does **not** store personal identifiers across sessions for profiling, and focuses on aggregated statistics (e.g. page views, referrers, devices, countries at an aggregated level).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used in order to improve it, without unnecessary interference with your rights.

Because no cookies or similar storage on your device are used for analytics, a consent banner is not required for this tool under typical ePrivacy / TTDSG assessments for cookieless analytics. You can still object to processing based on legitimate interest by contacting us (see section 10).

Further information about Plausible’s approach: https://plausible.io/data-policy

#### 4. Contact form

If you use the contact form, we process the data you submit (name, email address, message) in order to receive and respond to your enquiry.

- Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to a contract / handling your request) and/or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to enquiries).
- Retention: we keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, unless longer retention is required by law.

Form submissions are emailed via **Resend** (Resend, Inc.), which acts as a processor to deliver messages to our inbox. Resend may process and store message content and related logs in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses) under their Data Processing Addendum:
