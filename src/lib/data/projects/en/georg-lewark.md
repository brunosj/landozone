We built a minimalist portfolio for [Georg Lewark](https://georglewark.de/), a Berlin-based filmmaker. The design steps back to let the work speak: a clean project grid leads to individual film pages with embedded Vimeo and YouTube players, alongside an about page and a simple contact form.

The site is a lightweight SvelteKit app written in TypeScript. Projects are written in Markdown, so a new film can go live in minutes, without the overhead of a CMS.
