We built the website of [SHUSH](https://shush.dance), a Berlin-based sound system, music community and label. It is the collective's home online: upcoming events, the label's releases with an integrated audio player, artist pages and a merch store all live in one place.

Under the hood, it is a Next.js app with Payload CMS running on the same server. Checkout for tickets, music and merch is handled with Stripe and PayPal, and a custom dashboard in the admin tracks ticket and music sales per event and tier, with Excel export.
